# Awesome Windows

Windows applications and utilities for development, media, communication, productivity, security, and system management. Open-source and paid labels preserve the fixed source snapshot’s descriptions; unmarked entries do not imply free or closed-source software. Features, availability, pricing, and evaluative claims reflect that snapshot.

The upstream maintainers exclude tools they describe as “vibecoded” and require listed tools to meet their “awesome” selection standard.

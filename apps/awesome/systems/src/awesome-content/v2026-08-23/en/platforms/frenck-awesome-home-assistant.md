# Awesome Home Assistant

[Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io) is open-source home automation that prioritizes local control and privacy. This list covers installation, communities, public configurations, custom integrations, dashboard cards, themes, apps, DIY projects, and learning resources. You can explore the interface in the [Home Assistant online demo](https://demo.home-assistant.io).

The fixed source describes Home Assistant as running on your own hardware, supporting thousands of devices out of the box, and requiring no coding to set up, from a single room to a whole house. It describes the setup as keeping everything local and private.

According to the fixed source, most listed items can be installed in one click through [HACS](https://hacs.xyz), the Home Assistant Community Store, after installing Home Assistant itself. The source identifies the [Open Home Foundation](https://www.openhomefoundation.org) as Home Assistant's owner and the steward of ESPHome, Music Assistant, Z-Wave JS, and open voice tools. It also points to the [Works with Home Assistant](https://works-with.home-assistant.io) program for device testing covering privacy, local control, and long-term support.

Entries within categories are not ranked. Descriptions of activity, popularity, and star counts reflect the fixed source, rather than a current check.

## How to use <a id="contents"></a>

Use the list to find community-recommended apps, custom cards, integrations, and tutorials for getting started or planning another project.

- Press Command/Ctrl + F to search for a keyword.
- Use this page's contents navigation to jump to a category.
- Search the original list on [Awesome Home Assistant](https://www.awesome-ha.com).

## Installing

The fixed source suggests [Home Assistant Green](https://www.home-assistant.io/green/) as a straightforward starting point. You can also use hardware you already own, such as a Raspberry Pi, Mini PC, or old laptop, following the official guides below. Each option runs Home Assistant; after setup, install [HACS](https://hacs.xyz) to install most items in this list with one click, as described by the source.

- [Home Assistant installation](https://www.home-assistant.io/installation/) - Official installation guides.
- [Compare installation methods](https://www.home-assistant.io/installation/#compare-installation-methods) - Comparison of the available installation methods.

## Custom Integrations

Integrations Home Assistant does not ship with out of the box, written by the community. Install them through HACS in a couple of clicks.

### AI & LLMs <a id="-ai--llms"></a>

Wire Home Assistant up to a large language model and let it read your devices, build dashboards, write automations, or describe what your cameras see.

- [LLM Vision](https://github.com/valentinfrlch/ha-llmvision) - Add visual intelligence to your automations: caption camera snapshots, summarize what is happening, react to specific events (1,373 stars).
- [AI Automation Suggester](https://github.com/ITSpecialist111/ai_automation_suggester) - Scan your entities and ask an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, Ollama) for tailored automation suggestions, surfaced as notifications (749 stars).

### Lighting <a id="-lighting"></a>

Effects, schedules, and behaviour layers that sit on top of your lights.

- [Circadian Lighting](https://github.com/claytonjn/hass-circadian_lighting) - Slowly synchronizes your color-changing lights with the naturally occurring color temperature of the sky throughout the day (886 stars).
- [Adaptive Lighting](https://github.com/basnijholt/adaptive-lighting) - Slowly adjust the brightness and color temperature of your lights based on the position of the sun (3,324 stars).
- [Govee](https://github.com/LaggAt/hacs-govee) - Local control of Govee Wi-Fi lights and bulbs, including effects and color modes (352 stars).

### Climate <a id="️-climate"></a>

Smarter thermostats, comfort sensors, and HVAC integrations that go beyond what comes built in.

- [Better Thermostat](https://github.com/KartoffelToby/better_thermostat) - Smarter thermostat with window detection, heating curves, and per-room comfort profiles for thermostatic radiator valves (1,443 stars).
- [Versatile Thermostat](https://github.com/jmcollin78/versatile_thermostat) - Full-featured thermostat with presets, window detection, motion-based comfort, and presence (1,075 stars).
- [Midea Air Appliances LAN](https://github.com/nbogojevic/homeassistant-midea-air-appliances-lan) - Local control of Midea air conditioners, dehumidifiers, and other appliances over LAN (460 stars).
- [Smart Autotune Thermostat (SAT)](https://github.com/Alexwijn/SAT) - Self-tuning thermostat that talks to OpenTherm, ESPHome, or MQTT gateways and adapts the heating curve to your home over time (246 stars).
- [Dual Smart Thermostat](https://github.com/swingerman/ha-dual-smart-thermostat) - Enhanced version of the built-in generic thermostat, with separate heating and cooling, floor temperature limits, and humidity controls (225 stars).

## Alternative Home Automation Software

Options for comparing platforms beyond Home Assistant or meeting needs it does not cover. These include commercial and open-source projects that address different problems.

- [openHAB](https://github.com/openhab) - Java-based and aims at being a universal integration platform.

- [Domoticz](https://github.com/domoticz/domoticz) - A lightweight Home Automation System (3,773 stars).

- [Gladys](https://github.com/GladysAssistant/Gladys) - Open source program which runs on your Raspberry Pi (3,066 stars).

- [SmartThings](https://www.smartthings.com/) - Commercial home automation hub by Samsung.

- [Homey](https://homey.app/) - Commercial multi-protocol platform from Athom that puts local control first with Homey Pro, with Homey Cloud as a hosted option.

- [Homebridge](https://github.com/homebridge/homebridge) - Lightweight Node.js server that bridges non-HomeKit devices into Apple Home, with a large plugin ecosystem (25,384 stars).

- [Node-RED](https://github.com/node-red/node-red) - Flow-based visual programming tool for wiring together devices, APIs, and services, backed by the OpenJS Foundation (23,277 stars).

- [ioBroker](https://github.com/ioBroker/ioBroker) - Integration platform for IoT with hundreds of adapters and strong European protocol support for KNX and HomeMatic (1,371 stars).

- [FHEM](https://fhem.de/) - Perl-based server with 430+ modules and deep support for European protocols like KNX, EnOcean, and HomeMatic.

- [Jeedom](https://github.com/jeedom/core) - French-origin, cloudless platform with a plugin marketplace, running on any Linux system (414 stars).

- [Hubitat](https://hubitat.com/) - Commercial local-first hub supporting Z-Wave, Zigbee, and Matter with all automations running on the device.

- [HomeSeer](https://homeseer.com/) - Commercial platform with 25+ years of development, strong Z-Wave support, and an extensive plugin ecosystem.

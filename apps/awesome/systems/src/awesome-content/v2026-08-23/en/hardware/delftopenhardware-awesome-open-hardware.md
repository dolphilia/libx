# Awesome Open Hardware

Explore open-source hardware projects for automation, manufacturing, consumer devices, development boards, and scientific instruments. Talks, papers, conferences, platforms, podcasts, books, and training programs support making and learning about open hardware, with further reading and related lists.

## Projects

### Automation, Manufacturing, and Robotics

* [FarmBot](https://farm.bot/pages/open-source) - Automated gardening machine to grow vegetables.
* [Hacker Fab](https://docs.hackerfab.org/home) - Nanofabrication tools for making integrated circuits.
* [JPL Open Source Rover](https://github.com/nasa-jpl/open-source-rover) - A six-wheeled, build-it-yourself, open-source rover.
* [Mekanika](https://www.mekanika.io/) - Tools and machines for makers.
* [Millennium Machines](https://www.millennium-machines.com/) - A project dedicated to designing desktop CNC mills.
* [Open Source Ecology](https://www.opensourceecology.org/) - Open-source industrial machines.
* [Opulo](https://docs.opulo.io/) - A project developing a pick-and-place machine.
* [Precious Plastic](https://www.preciousplastic.com/) - Tools to make plastic recycling as simple as possible.
* [Prusa3D](https://www.prusa3d.com/) - A 3D printer manufacturer that shares its work openly online.
* [RepRap](https://reprap.org/wiki/RepRap) - A general-purpose, self-replicating manufacturing machine, described by the source as humanity’s first.
* [VORON Design](https://www.vorondesign.com/) - 3D printer designs described by the source as no-compromise.
* [WikiHouse](https://www.wikihouse.cc/) - Digitally designed open housing.

### Consumer Devices

* [freeDSP](https://freedsp.github.io) - A family of audio digital signal processors.
* [Input Labs](https://inputlabs.io) - Video game controllers featuring gyroscopic sensors.
* [Mutable Instruments](https://mutable-instruments.net/) - Eurorack classics.
* [PiKVM](https://pikvm.org/) - DIY IP-KVM based on Raspberry Pi, described by the source as inexpensive.
* [PiSpot Show](https://github.com/GeiserX/PiSpot-Show) - A Raspberry Pi appliance that drives HDMI displays as self-updating Wi-Fi voucher kiosks with live weather.
* [PiSpot Watch](https://github.com/GeiserX/PiSpot-Watch) - Wrist-wearable Raspberry Pi Zero smartwatch with e-ink display that generates Wi-Fi voucher codes on demand.
* [SlimeVR](https://slimevr.dev) - Full-body tracking sensors for virtual reality and motion capture.
* [Winterbloom](https://winterbloom.com) - Eurorack modules, with design write-ups by Thea on her blog.

### Development Boards

* [Arduino](https://www.arduino.cc/) - An electronics platform.
* [OpenMV](https://github.com/openmv/openmv) - A machine vision platform described by the source as low-cost.
* [pic0rick](https://github.com/kelu124/pic0rick/) - A Raspberry Pi Pico platform for pulse-echo ultrasound imaging hardware.

### Scientific Instruments

* [Biohack Academy](https://assets.waag.org/biohackacademy) - Biotechnological hardware such as an incubator, thermocycler, centrifuge, microscope, etc.
* [GaudiLab](http://www.gaudi.ch/GaudiLabs/?page_id=19) - Hardware projects for biology laboratories.
* [mesoSPIM](http://mesospim.org/) - Light-sheet microscopes for imaging in cleared tissue.
* [Open Gamma Detector](https://github.com/Open-Gamma-Project/Open-Gamma-Detector) - A hackable gamma-ray spectrometer described by the source as low-cost.
* [OpenBCI](https://openbci.com/) - Brain-computer interface.
* [OpenFlexure](https://openflexure.org/) - 3D-printed microscope with a precise mechanical stage.
* [OpenSPIM](https://openspim.org/) - Open-access platform for three-dimensional (3D) microscopy.
* [openUC2](https://github.com/openUC2/UC2-GIT) - Modular microscopy toolbox.
* [SafeCast](https://safecast.org/) - Environmental measurements for the public domain.

## Conferences

* [FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org) - An open-source event described by the source as held annually in Brussels.
* [Open Hardware Summit](https://oshwa.org/events/) - An open hardware conference described by the source as annual.
* [Open Sauce](https://opensauce.com/) - A science and technology convention described by the source as annual, with dates of 17–19 July; no year is specified.
* [Maker Faire](https://makerfaire.com/) - A celebration of the Maker Movement, locally organized.
* [Hackaday Supercon](https://hackaday.io/superconference/) - A conference on hardware hacking and in-depth technical workshops, described by the source as annual.
* [KiCon](https://kicon.kicad.org/) - Conferences about KiCad open-source EDA, described by the source as held annually in different regions.
* [RISC-V Summits](https://riscv.org/community/risc-v-summits/) - Summits organized by RISC-V International, described by the source as held annually in different regions.
* [World RISC-V Days](https://riscv.org/world-risc-v-days/) - Synchronized global events voluntarily organized by RISC-V communities worldwide.
* [Latch-Up](https://fossi-foundation.org/latch-up) - A three-day conference dedicated to free and open-source silicon, described by the source as held in North America every spring.
* [ORConf](https://fossi-foundation.org/orconf) - Another three-day conference dedicated to free and open-source silicon, described by the source as held in Europe every autumn.

## Platforms

* [Open Hardware Repository](https://ohwr.org/about) - For electronics designers at experimental physics facilities to collaborate on open hardware designs.
* [Open Hardware Observatory](https://en.oho.wiki/wiki/Home) - Free blueprints for sustainable open hardware.
* [Instructables](https://www.instructables.com/circuits/projects) - Instructables is a place that lets you explore, document, and share your creations.
* [Hackaday.io](https://hackaday.io/) - A collaborative hardware development community described by the source as the world’s largest.
* [Hackster.io](https://www.hackster.io/about) - A platform for connecting developers.
* [Openhardware.io](https://www.openhardware.io/about) - Tracks open hardware projects.
* [Openhardware.science](https://openhardware.science/) - An initiative with the source-stated goal of making open science hardware ubiquitous by 2025.
* [Openlifescience](https://openlifesci.org/) - A program to develop Open Science ambassadors in research.
* [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/) - A platform for sharing and contributing hardware designs for 3D printing, laser cutting, and CNC milling.
* [Kitspace.org](https://kitspace.org) - A place to build and share electronics projects.

## Further Readings

* [Open Source Guide](https://opensource.guide/) - A website created by GitHub to help people work on open source projects.
* [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_hardware) - General information about open-source hardware.
* [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/) - Enables groups to quickly set up a collective, raise funds and manage them transparently.
* [The Journal of Open Hardware](https://openhardware.metajnl.com/) - A peer-reviewed, open-access publication for open hardware research and development (JOH).
* [OSHWA Certification](https://certification.oshwa.org/) - A way for producers to indicate that their products meet a uniform, well-defined standard for open-source compliance.
* [HardwareX Journal](https://www.journals.elsevier.com/hardwarex) - An open-access scientific hardware journal.

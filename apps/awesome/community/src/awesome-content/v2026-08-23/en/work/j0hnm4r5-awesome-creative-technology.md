# Awesome Creative Technology

Creative technology combines computing, design, art, and user experience across disciplines. This list brings together companies, studios, collectives, schools, festivals, and conferences around the world as sources of inspiration and references for potential employers and creative technology meetups.

Creative technologists often have broad skills rather than a single specialty. The categories indicate each group’s general focus; they are not a strict or complete classification.

The tables preserve the locations and links recorded in the source. In the Jobs column, Email and Website indicate the contact method; a blank cell does not establish whether a group is hiring. Incomplete location labels and links pointing to other organisations are marked as source entries rather than independently verified details.

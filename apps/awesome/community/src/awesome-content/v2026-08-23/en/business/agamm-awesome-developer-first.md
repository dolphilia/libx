# Awesome Developer-First

Developer-first products target developers, often through APIs, headless services, or SaaS tools, and usually present code examples on their front pages. This list covers coding assistants, authentication, deployment, data services, monitoring, payments, and testing, including paid products, open-source tools, and free plans. Descriptions, comparisons, and plan limits follow the fixed upstream snapshot.

* [Aider](https://aider.chat/) - Open-source terminal AI assistant. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider)
* [Amazon Q](https://aws.amazon.com/q/developer/) - Amazon’s AI assistant.

* [CodeSquire](https://codesquire.ai) - Browser extension for AI generation in environments such as Jupyter and BigQuery.

* [Stytch](https://stytch.com/) - All-in-one authentication, authorization, and fraud prevention infrastructure.
* [SuperTokens](https://supertokens.io/) - Open-source and hosted email and OAuth authentication with a prebuilt UI. [GitHub repository](https://github.com/supertokens/supertokens-core)

* [WorkOS](https://workos.com/) - Enterprise SSO and more out-of-the-box enterprise features.

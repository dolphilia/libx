# Awesome Hacker News Tools of the Trade

A catalog of development and business tools assembled from [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com), AngelList, and Quora, including hosted and self-hosted services. The tables compare tools for software development, operations, marketing, payments, and business administration.

The list grew from [Joshua Schachter’s 2010 discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1769910) and [Sharjeel Qureshi’s 2013 discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=5235137), and was expanded for 2015 and beyond. The 2010 question concerned hosted alternatives to tools such as CVS, email, and mailing lists, and excluded production services such as EC2 and Heroku; the expanded list includes production platforms and self-hosted tools.

Prices, service names, capabilities, and vendor claims reflect the recorded source edition. Hosting tags distinguish `Hosted` services from `Self-hosted` software.

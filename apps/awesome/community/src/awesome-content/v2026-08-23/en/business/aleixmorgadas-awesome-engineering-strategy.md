# Awesome Engineering Strategy

Resources for technology leaders designing and executing engineering strategies: books, articles, videos, methods, courses, workshops, and templates. Supporting materials, public engineering strategies, communities, and podcasts provide ways to learn from others and put strategy into practice.

## Books

- [Strategic Monoliths and Microservices: Driving Innovation Using Purposeful Architecture](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55782292-strategic-monoliths-and-microservices) - Strategic patterns for addressing business capabilities through monoliths and microservices.
- [Technology Strategy Patterns: Architecture as Strategy](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/42414767-technology-strategy-patterns) - Business tools and patterns from an engineering perspective.
- [Staff Engineer: Leadership Beyond the Management Track](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/56481725-staff-engineer) - The staff engineer role and how it contributes to engineering strategy.
- [Reaching Cloud Velocity: A Leader's Guide to Success in the AWS Cloud](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/53503300-reaching-cloud-velocity) - A high-level guide to business strategy, people, culture, data, and architecture, and how they drive cloud velocity.
- [UN Global Platform Handbook on Information Technology Strategy](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55047345-un-global-platform-handbook-on-information-technology-strategy) - Wardley Maps for IT, bringing situational awareness to different contexts.
- [Good Strategy Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/11721966) - The core components of a strategy.
- [The Value Flywheel Effect: Power the Future and Accelerate Your Organization to the Modern Cloud](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/61167316-the-value-flywheel-effect?ac=1&from_search=true&qid=donxleC2tR&rank=1) - Wardley Mapping and other sensemaking approaches to help organizations anticipate market changes and user needs.
- [Team Topologies: Organizing Business and Technology Teams for Fast Flow](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44135420-team-topologies) - A practical, step-by-step, adaptive model for organizational design and team interaction, based on four fundamental team types and three interaction patterns.
- [Thinking in Systems](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3828902-thinkingow-in-systems) - An introduction to systems thinking, covering its fundamentals, several system traps, and ways to overcome them.
- [Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/179133.Domain_Driven_Design) - Strategic patterns for understanding complex domains and modeling them according to their impact on business competitive advantage.
- [Implementing Domain-Driven Design](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/15756865-implementing-domain-driven-design) - An approach to implementing DDD, from strategic to tactical patterns, with code examples.
- [Domain-Driven Design Distilled](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/28602719-domain-driven-design-distilled) - An accessible guide to the main concepts of Domain-Driven Design.
- [Wardley Maps](https://medium.com/wardleymaps/on-being-lost-2ef5f05eb1ec) - An in-depth explanation of the origins of Wardley Maps and how to develop strategy that adapts to its environment.
- [Architecture Modernization. Socio-technical alignment of software, strategy, and structure](https://www.manning.com/books/architecture-modernization) - Concrete tools, techniques, and processes for aligning software architecture with business domains, organizational design, team topologies, and corporate strategy.
- [Effective Platform Engineering](https://www.manning.com/books/effective-platform-engineering) - Designing, building, deploying, and scaling custom internal development platforms (IDPs).

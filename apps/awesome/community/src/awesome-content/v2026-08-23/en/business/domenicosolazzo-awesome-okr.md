# Awesome OKR

A collection of learning resources, research, templates, examples, and tools for Objectives and Key Results (OKRs). OKRs help companies, teams, and employees set and communicate objectives, measure progress through achieved results, and align their work with the company’s mission and vision. The recorded source cites Google, Netflix, Zynga, LinkedIn, and Twitter as examples of companies using OKRs.

## Research

Research papers connected to OKRs and goal-setting theory.

- [The Determinants of Goal Commitment](http://amr.aom.org/content/13/1/23.abstract): A discussion of the concept and measurement of goal commitment, a key aspect of goal-setting theory. The paper argues that the strength of the relationship between commitment and performance depends on the amount of variation in commitment.
- [Goal Setting and Task Performance: 1969-1980](http://datause.cse.ucla.edu/docs/eal_goa_1981.pdf): Goal setting is most likely to improve task performance when goals are specific and sufficiently challenging; participants have sufficient ability and differences in ability are controlled; feedback shows progress relative to the goal; rewards, such as money, are given for goal attainment; the experimenter or manager is supportive; and the individual accepts assigned goals.
